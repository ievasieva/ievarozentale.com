## 4. APPROACH (approach.html)

**Page title:** Approach | Ieva Rozentale — Facilitation Consultant Australia
**Meta description:** My approach combines facilitation, behavioural insights, narrative approaches, and reflective practices to help groups explore complex problems and develop thoughtful responses.

### Page header
Breadcrumb: Home / Approach
Heading: How I Work
Lead: My work combines facilitation, behavioural insights, narrative approaches, and reflective practices.

### Overview
These methods help groups explore complex problems, engage with uncertainty, and develop thoughtful responses.

### What Shapes My Practice
Section label: Influences
Heading: What Shapes My Practice
Intro: My approach draws on several traditions:

Influence — Facilitation & Participatory Methods: Creating structured spaces where diverse perspectives can be explored. Good facilitation design ensures that every voice can contribute and that groups can move through complex terrain together.
Influence — Behavioural Insights: Understanding how human decision-making and group dynamics influence outcomes. Drawing on behavioural science helps design processes that work with how people actually think and behave, rather than how we assume they do.
Influence — Narrative Approaches: Exploring how organisations understand their challenges and possible futures. Narrative methods help surface the stories and assumptions that shape how we see problems and what we believe is possible.
Influence — Theory U & Reflective Practices: Supporting deeper listening and awareness in collective processes. Theory U offers a framework for understanding how groups can move from reactive to more generative and creative responses.
Influence — Futures Thinking: Exploring possible future developments and their implications for present decisions. Futures thinking helps organisations develop more adaptive and resilient strategies by expanding their view of what might be possible.

[DADA] "The map is not the territory. But a good map helps you wander well."

### How These Come Together
Section label: In practice
Heading: How These Come Together
In practice, I draw on these traditions fluidly depending on what a particular group or challenge needs. Some situations call for structured problem-solving; others require space for deeper reflection and dialogue.
My goal is always to create processes that are well-designed, genuinely engaging, and that help groups move forward with greater clarity and confidence.
Highlight block: The best facilitation is often invisible — participants are so engaged with the substance of their work that the process itself recedes into the background.
